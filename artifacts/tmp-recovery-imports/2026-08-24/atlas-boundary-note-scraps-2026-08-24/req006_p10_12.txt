PRIORITY MODEL


Build integrity before scale
The development direction contains nine major workstreams. Their sequencing is deliberate: first
make the local lifecycle truthful and durable; then operationalize evidence and integrations; only then
distribute the system.

P0 - foundational                                                                                                                        3

                             Analyzer ecosystem. registry, normalized schema, capabilities/resources, isolation.
                             Integration. RabbitMQ semantics, REST, MCP, webhooks and event adapters.



    P2                       Distributed scale. PostgreSQL projections, object/CAS storage, worker pools, quotas.
                             Operator surface. Job Status Surface, review queue, artifact/evidence graph, provenance viewer.

    DEFINITION OF DONE

    Intake state, identity, deduplication, configuration, progress, and failure policy all have one durable meaning that
    is shared by the CLI, embedded API, tests, workers, and future remote interfaces.




Why this must precede scale
Distributing workers or adding APIs before semantic integrity would replicate ambiguity across components. P0-1
makes the local engine authoritative enough to become a service safely.
