EXECUTIVE BRIEF


The development direction in one view
ATLAS is evolving from a compact local artifact-processing kernel into a governed control plane that
can take raw or untrusted material and preserve a reproducible chain from source, to exact bytes, to
structure, to analysis, to evidence, to an explicit downstream trust decision.


    The differentiator is not that ATLAS can run six Python stages. The
    differentiator is that it can make artifact identity, provenance, safety, evidence,
    and promotion decisions part of the execution model itself.

The key semantic correction: occurrence is not identity
ATLAS must treat a filesystem path as an occurrence and a digest as the identity of the bytes. The same bytes can
appear in a repository, a ZIP, a backup, an email attachment, and a forensic image. Those locations should remain
separately traceable while converging on one content identity.


    W H Y I T M AT T E R S

    Persistent occurrence-to-content relationships enable true deduplication, repeat-analysis avoidance, lineage
    queries, migration validation, and evidence defensibility without collapsing distinct provenance into one record.




Immutable intake as a trust anchor
If fingerprinting re-discovers a live filesystem instead of consuming the exact inventory observed during
Reconnaissance, the source can change between phases. The long-term design therefore treats Reconnaissance as a
snapshot authority: downstream phases operate on the recorded occurrence set, not a fresh traversal.

Hardening has already changed the trajectory
The source describes an earlier architecture review that exposed fail-open handler wiring, structural-discovery/
extraction conflation, TAR safety issues, event persistence gaps, duplicate-key behavior, serialization problems, and
configuration issues. Subsequent code work addressed several of those findings. That assessment-to-hardening
feedback loop should remain part of the project culture.


    S T R AT E G I C A D VA N TA G E

    ATLAS is still small enough that semantics can be corrected before distributed infrastructure and broad
    integrations make those mistakes expensive to unwind.

  • Repeatability: jobs can be reconstructed from immutable intake and versioned analyzer evidence.

Engineering decision rule

provenance, recovery, policy consistency, or evidence quality. ATLAS's strategic differentiation depends on those
properties remaining stronger than its orchestration novelty.
