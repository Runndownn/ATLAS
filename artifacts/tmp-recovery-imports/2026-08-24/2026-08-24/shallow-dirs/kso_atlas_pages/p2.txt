EXECUTIVE BRIEF


The development direction in one view
ATLAS is evolving from a compact local artifact-processing kernel into a governed control plane that
can take raw or untrusted material and preserve a reproducible chain from source, to exact bytes, to
structure, to analysis, to evidence, to an explicit downstream trust decision.


    The differentiator is not that ATLAS can run six Python stages. The
    differentiator is that it can make artifact identity, provenance, safety, evidence,
    and promotion decisions part of the execution model itself.

    E N D - S TAT E T H E S I S

    ATLAS should become an artifact lifecycle control plane, not another generic workflow or agent
    framework. Agents, LLMs, parsers, security tools, and domain analyzers can operate inside governed phases;
    ATLAS remains authoritative for lifecycle state, provenance, safety, evidence lineage, policy, and promotion.
