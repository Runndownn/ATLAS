CLOSING DIRECTION


Protect the part of ATLAS that is genuinely
differentiated
    Do not allow ATLAS to become another agent framework. Preserve
    deterministic artifact progression as the core abstraction.

ATLAS has a legitimate architectural reason to exist because it can become the reusable, observable,
evidence-aware boundary between raw or untrusted artifacts and trusted downstream systems.


Protect these invariants                                               Use scale to amplify - not redefine
  • The six-phase lifecycle remains explicit and ordered.                • Workers distribute execution, not authority.
  • Exact content identity is distinct from occurrence/location.         • PostgreSQL scales state, not meaning.
  • Materialization is controlled and isolated.                          • CAS/object storage scales content, not provenance
  • Analyzer output is not automatically trusted.                          semantics.

  • Evidence and promotion decisions are durable and                     • MCP/REST widen access, not privilege.
    attributable.                                                        • AI increases analytical capability, not trust authority.
  • External APIs and agents cannot bypass lifecycle policy.             • UI surfaces decisions; it does not become the source of
                                                                           truth.



    DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION

    Build ATLAS into a governed artifact-processing substrate. Finish semantic integrity and durable control
    first; operationalize evidence and analyzers second; scale infrastructure third. The payoff is one shared lifecycle
    that BinReaper, RUDI-K, repository analysis, forensics, malware/network analysis, corpus curation, supply-chain
    workflows, and future agents can reuse without independently rebuilding identity, safety, provenance, and trust
    controls.
