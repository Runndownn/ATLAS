    WHAT THIS DIAGRAM REPRESENTS

    This mind map shows ATLAS as a central control-plane concept with six capability domains radiating outward: Identity,
    Safety, Lifecycle, Evidence, Integrations, and Use Cases. The purpose of the visual is to show that the platform is
    defined not by one tool or one workflow, but by the interaction between provenance, controlled execution, governance,
    and extensibility. Each branch represents a family of responsibilities that can grow over time while remaining anchored
    to the same lifecycle and trust model at the center. The map also helps stakeholders see where future features belong,
    which reduces architectural drift and prevents important capabilities from being treated as disconnected add-ons.




        DESIGN IMPLICATION

        Use this view when aligning stakeholders, scoping roadmaps, or explaining why ATLAS should be built as a governed platform
        rather than a point solution.
