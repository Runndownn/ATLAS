The lineage view shows the target evidence chain that lets a trusted downstream publication be traced back to the exact

    TARGET PROVENANCE AND EVIDENCE CHAIN

                                Trace both directions: source -> publication and publication -> exact source bytes.




            SourceRoot                          IntakeGeneration               ArtifactOccurrence                     ContentIdentity

           registered source                     stable observation             where bytes appeared                   canonical digest




          AnalysisFinding                       EvidenceRecord                 PromotionDecision                 DestinationPublication

           normalized output                    meaning + confidence            approve / reject / hold               trusted destination




    WHAT THIS DIAGRAM REPRESENTS

    This diagram shows the target traceability chain that allows ATLAS to follow information from a registered source
    through to a final downstream publication. SourceRoot and IntakeGeneration establish where and when material was
    observed, while ArtifactOccurrence and ContentIdentity deliberately separate location from the exact bytes that were
    processed. AnalysisFinding and EvidenceRecord capture what a tool, parser, security engine, or model concluded and
    how that conclusion is characterized, including meaning, provenance, and confidence where applicable.
    PromotionDecision records the governance step that determines whether the evidence or artifact is approved, rejected,
    or held for further review. DestinationPublication closes the chain by recording where approved material was sent,
    enabling full bidirectional lineage from a trusted output back to the original observed bytes.




        DESIGN IMPLICATION

        This model is the foundation for reproducibility, deduplication without provenance loss, auditability, evidence review, and
        trustworthy knowledge publication.
