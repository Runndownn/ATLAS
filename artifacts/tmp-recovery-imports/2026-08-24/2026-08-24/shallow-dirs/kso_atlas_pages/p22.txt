AI AND AGENT GOVERNANCE


AI belongs inside the lifecycle - not above it
    The model should be able to analyze, classify, correlate, and propose. It
    should not become the authority over source provenance, filesystem safety,
    durable control, or final promotion.


Appropriate AI roles                                                    ATLAS retains authority
  • Classify artifact type or domain.                                     • Register and freeze the source occurrence set.
  • Extract structured facts from documents or technical notes.           • Establish exact content identity.
  • Propose relationships between findings.                               • Enforce archive and path safety.
  • Summarize analyzer output into evidence candidates.                   • Control workspace materialization.
  • Recommend routing to specialized analyzers.                           • Persist lifecycle and event history.
  • Propose a promotion decision with supporting rationale.               • Apply review/policy gates and record publication.
