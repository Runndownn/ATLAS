    ARCHITECTURAL ROLE

    ATLAS is best positioned as the governed ingestion and artifact-processing layer underneath security
    systems, forensic workflows, repository intelligence, knowledge pipelines, corpus curation, and AI-assisted
    analysis.




Why the boundary matters
Generic orchestration answers "which task runs next?" ATLAS is intended to answer a more evidence-sensitive
question: what happened to this exact content, under which controls, and why was a result ever trusted?
