    WHAT THIS DIAGRAM REPRESENTS

    This river flow chart shows ATLAS as a stream that begins with many raw inputs and becomes narrower as uncertainty
    is reduced through intake, identity, analysis, and review. The tributaries depict the fact that different source types can
    enter the system, but they are brought into a common controlled channel rather than handled as separate ad hoc
    pipelines. Each stage along the river represents progressive normalization and governance, meaning the system is
    always converting noisy observations into more structured and defensible information. By the time the flow reaches
    trusted output, the visual makes clear that downstream publication is not a direct consequence of ingestion, but the
    result of staged processing and explicit control.




        DESIGN IMPLICATION

        Use this view to explain ATLAS to non-specialists because it emphasizes progressive reduction of uncertainty and the
        disciplined movement from raw source material to trustworthy output.
