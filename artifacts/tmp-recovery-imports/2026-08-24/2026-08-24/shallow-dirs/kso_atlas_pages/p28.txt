intent from logs, filenames, or model output.

                                                                  normalized finding.




   Was the claim observed or inferred?                            Who or what approved it?

   evidence.                                                      review authority.




   Where was it published?                                        Can the conclusion be reproduced?

    Successful completion means ATLAS can preserve the chain from raw source
    and exact bytes, through every structural and analytical operation, to the
    evidence produced, the decision applied, and every trusted destination that
    received it.
