Operational advantages once hardened
  • Repeatability: jobs can be reconstructed from immutable intake and versioned analyzer evidence.
  • Deduplication without provenance loss: identical bytes converge on one identity while all occurrences remain traceable.
  • Safer automation: structural inspection and resource budgets precede extraction and analysis.
  • Cross-domain reuse: security, repository, document, forensic, and AI analyzers share one lifecycle.
  • Decision traceability: approved knowledge can be traced back through evidence, findings, and exact source bytes.
  • Incremental scale: the same semantics can move from local SQLite execution to workers and service interfaces later.


    MOST DEFENSIBLE POSITIONING

    ATLAS is infrastructure for governed artifact progression. It is valuable when raw input is untrusted,
    repeated, nested, evidentiary, AI-analyzed, or destined for a trusted system.
