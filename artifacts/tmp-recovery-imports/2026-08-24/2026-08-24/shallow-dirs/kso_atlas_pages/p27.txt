    CRITICAL SEQUENCING CONSTRAINT

    Do not prioritize a large web UI, multi-agent orchestration, or distributed workers before the P0 semantics are
    finished. Those features would scale current ambiguity instead of resolving it.




Engineering decision rule
When a proposed feature competes with a lifecycle-integrity requirement, prefer the requirement that improves
provenance, recovery, policy consistency, or evidence quality. ATLAS's strategic differentiation depends on those
properties remaining stronger than its orchestration novelty.
