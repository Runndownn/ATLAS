CURRENT ARCHITECTURE


A compact, readable execution kernel
The current design is small by intent. AtlasRuntime centralizes construction; PipelineOrchestrator
advances the lifecycle; SQLite records jobs and phases; safety and hashing services support phase
work; the event bus decouples observers from execution.
