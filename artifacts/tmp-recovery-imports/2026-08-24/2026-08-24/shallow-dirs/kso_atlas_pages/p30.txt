    WHAT THIS DIAGRAM REPRESENTS

    This diagram shows ATLAS maturing through staged capability, from the current local lifecycle kernel to a hardened
    local engine, then to a durable service, governed control plane, distributed scale-out, and finally agentic integration.
    Each stage adds infrastructure and operational reach without changing the A-F lifecycle semantics that define identity,
    safety, analysis, evidence, and trust. Semantic integrity, durable state, recovery behavior, and control requests come
    before service interfaces and distributed workers so unresolved local behavior is not multiplied across more
    components. Governance is introduced before broad scale because persistent evidence, review decisions, quarantine,
    and policy gates are what make larger deployments defensible. Agentic integration appears last because agents
    should select or parameterize governed workflows, not replace ATLAS as the authority over provenance, policy,
    lifecycle state, or final promotion.


        DESIGN IMPLICATION

        Treat scale as an infrastructure change, not a semantic redesign; every later stage should preserve the same provenance and
        trust contracts proven locally.
